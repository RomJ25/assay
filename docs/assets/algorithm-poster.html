The Conviction Algorithm

A transparent, two-dimensional factor engine. Reported financials and market prices go in; a ranked, classified, gate-checked conviction list comes out — with zero forward-looking inputs in the ranking path.

S&P 500 · Russell 1000/ Value × Quality/ geometric mean/ rank, don't predict/ no ML · no black boxes
01

Universe & Filings

Input · point-in-time

Each rebalance pulls the constituent list and the latest annual statements into one FinancialData record per name — a normalized, multi-section record up to four years deep, read through a None-safe .get(field, year) so a single missing line never crashes the pipeline.

02

Two Independent Dimensions

Score · two 0–100 axes

Value and Quality are built from disjoint inputs — they share almost no raw fields — so the two scores carry genuinely independent information. Most metrics become 0–100 percentile ranks across the universe; the Piotroski F-Score is the exception — an absolute count of nine pass/fail tests, normalized to 100.

04

Three Downgrade Gates

Gate · candidate → watch list

Landing in the goal cell is necessary but not sufficient. Three independent vetoes can demote a research candidate back to the watch list — each catching a failure mode the threshold map can't see.

F-Score Gate
raw F < 6 → downgrade
Strong gross profitability can mask multiple simultaneous signs of financial deterioration.
Piotroski 2000
Momentum Gate
12-1m in bottom 25% → downgrade
Excludes the cheapest names the market is still actively dumping. A veto only — never a scoring axis.
Jegadeesh-Titman 1993 · RAFI 2024 (+2.8%/yr)
Revenue Gate
2+ yrs declining → downgrade
Persistent top-line decline is the clearest structural value-trap signal beyond earnings.
Chen, Chen, Hsin & Lee 2014
05
Rank & grade the survivors · complements, not substitutes
Conviction = √( Value × Quality )

The surviving candidates are ordered by a single score. The arithmetic mean would treat the axes as substitutes — a great price papering over a bad business. The geometric mean treats them as complements: by AM–GM it is maximized only when V = Q, so both must be strong.

and each survivor is graded — confidence = min(V−70, Q−70) → ▲ HIGH ≥15 · ● MODERATE ≥5 · ▼ LOW — the weakest axis sets the grade
Conviction and the matrix are two parallel reads of the same (V, Q) pair — the matrix assigns the type, conviction ranks the strength. Neither feeds the other.

Validation & Intellectual Honesty

the rigor behind the numbers
The SMCI Audit
+2.2%/yr · naive → −1.5%/yr · corrected
An April 2026 adversarial audit found the entire reported selection alpha came from survivorship bias — chiefly SMCI, scored in Q4 2023 though it didn't join the S&P 500 until March 2024. Correcting that one name flipped the edge negative. The biased mode survives only as --survivorship-naive.
Point-in-Time Backtest
  • Survivorship-free — actual index membership per quarter
  • 75-day filing lag — 10-K deadline + buffer, no look-ahead
  • Equal-weight — built for real individual portfolios
  • 10 bps round-trip cost · quarter-end rebalance
  • Two benchmarks — SPY and universe-EW selection alpha
Stated Limits
  • 4-yr sample — illustrative, not inferential
  • <30 quarters auto-warns in the report
  • 4 of 9 Piotroski conventions differ from academia — documented, not hidden
